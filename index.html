About the Artist

I work between three kinds of making: surreal portraits on canvas, large murals on walls and facades, and the quiet repair of old paintings and painted furniture.

Clouds and weather keep turning into faces in my work — a way of painting an inner life onto the sky. The same patience and respect for material that a mural needs is what an old, damaged canvas asks for too. I take a limited number of commissions and conservation projects so each can be given the time it deserves.
